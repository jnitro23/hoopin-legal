Hoopin uses SMS text messages solely to verify your phone number when you sign in or create an account ("the Program"). By entering your phone number and requesting a code, you consent to receive a one-time passcode (OTP) text message from Hoopin.

Message frequency varies and is limited to messages you trigger by requesting a sign-in code. Hoopin does not send recurring or marketing text messages as part of this Program.

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opt-out

Because messages are sent only in direct response to a sign-in attempt you initiate, there is no recurring subscription to cancel. If you no longer wish to receive verification texts, simply don't request a sign-in code via phone — you can sign in with Apple, Google, or email instead.
