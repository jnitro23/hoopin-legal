Hoopin SMS Verification Consent

How users consent to receive text messages from Hoopin

What we send

Hoopin is an iOS app for finding and organizing pickup basketball games. The only text message Hoopin sends is a single one-time verification code when a person chooses to sign in or sign up with their phone number. We do not send marketing or promotional text messages.

How users opt in

  1. On the welcome screen, the user taps "Continue with phone".
  2. On the "What's your number?" screen, the user enters their own mobile number.
  3. Directly beneath the "Send code" button, the following disclosure is displayed before the user taps the button:
By tapping "Send code", you agree to Hoopin's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy and consent to receive a one-time SMS verification code. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.
  1. The user taps "Send code" and receives one SMS containing a verification code, which they enter in the app to finish signing in.
