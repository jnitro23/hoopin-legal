Hoopin Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Hoopin ("we", "us", "our") operates the Hoopin mobile app, which helps basketball players find nearby courts, check in with friends, and organize pickup runs. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you create a Hoopin account, we collect your name, chosen sign-in method (Apple, Google, phone number, or email), profile photo, and playing position/skill level. When you use the app, we collect check-in activity, run participation, and friend connections you create.

Phone numbers and SMS

If you sign in with your phone number, we use it solely to send you a one-time verification code (OTP) to confirm it's you. We do not send marketing messages, and message frequency is limited to sign-in attempts you initiate.

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number with third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes. Your number may be shared with our SMS delivery provider (Twilio) solely to deliver verification codes on our behalf.

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on your sign-in activity.

How we use your information

We use your information to operate core app features: showing nearby courts, displaying check-in counts, notifying you about runs and friend activity, and maintaining your profile.

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that help us run the app (e.g. Supabase for authentication and data storage, Twilio for SMS delivery, push notification services), and only as needed to provide the service.

Your choices

You can update or delete your profile information from within the app, or contact us to request account deletion.
